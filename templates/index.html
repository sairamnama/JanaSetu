How GramSeva Works for Rural Citizens

A clear, transparent 4-step journey from civic problem to verified resolution.

📱

1. Submit Issue

Select civic category (Water, Power, Roads, Sanitation), provide location landmark, attach photo, and receive an instant Tracking ID.

🔍

2. Panchayat Verification

Panchayat Secretary reviews the complaint, verifies priority, and routes it to the designated department and field officer.

🛠️

3. Field Remediation

Assigned field officers (e.g. Line Inspector, Sanitary Supervisor) initiate on-site repairs with continuous status updates and remarks.

⭐

4. Citizen Rating

Once the work is completed, citizen reviews resolution proof and rates the quality of service from 1 to 5 stars.
